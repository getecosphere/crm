Eco starter composition

Build systems that can grow without losing their shape.

Eco turns Domain-Driven Design into a practical workspace: clear domains, explicit runtime contracts, and one composition layer that makes them feel like one application.

Chapter 01

Eco keeps the seams visible.

A scalable product is not one enormous codebase. It is a set of bounded contexts that can change at their own pace, composed deliberately at the edge. Eco keeps the operational details—repositories, runtimes, CTs, deployment, and service exposure—in that same deliberate shape.

Domains stay focused

Each repository owns one meaningful capability and its data contract.

Composition stays human

The project composition owns the experience that connects those capabilities.

Operations stay explicit

The estate manifest describes where services run and what they need.

Chapter 02

DDD is a conversation about boundaries.

Domains communicate through contracts. They do not reach into one another's implementation or database. The composition layer speaks to those contracts and turns them into a coherent customer journey.

Chapter 04

This starter is meant to be replaced.

crm_composition/frontend and, if selected, crm_composition/backend are runnable placeholders. Delete them when you are ready to create the actual project with the vibecoding model you choose.

Keep the composition contract.
Your future frontend remains the public entry point; your future backend remains a separately-owned API. Eco will continue to provision and run what the manifest declares.
